About Absolute Motion

Engineering Intelligence Into Every Motion

Headquartered in Rabale MIDC, Navi Mumbai, Absolute Motion Private Limited is one of India's trusted industrial automation solution providers and custom control panel manufacturers. With over a decade of hands-on engineering excellence, we specialize in solving complex production, packaging, and motion control challenges.

Our state-of-the-art facility integrates mechanical fabrication, laser-cut CNC panel enclosures, precision wiring, PLC/SCADA programming, and live Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT) under one roof. We operate platform-agnostic architectures across Siemens, Rockwell Automation, Omron, Schneider Electric, WAGO, EXOR, and Cognex Vision.
